Lab 2: Multivariate Visualization with D3

This visualization explores the relationship among city population, temperature, development level, and region.

Design Justification

I mapped population to the x-position and temperature to the y-position. Both are quantitative, and position is the visual channel people read most accurately, so putting the two numeric variables on the axes lets viewers judge magnitudes and spot a trend between them directly.

Development level is ordered (Low, Medium, High), so I encoded it with shapes (circle, square, triangle) which keeps the three categories distinguishable without competing with the axes. Region is purely nominal with four values, so color works well: hues have no implied order, and four is few enough to stay readable. Each point is also labeled with its city name, and a tooltip reports every attribute on hover for exact values.

I hope viewers notice the clear negative relationship between population and temperature, and that the larger, cooler cities are mostly high-development ones in the North and West, while the small, warm cities in the South tend to be low-development.
